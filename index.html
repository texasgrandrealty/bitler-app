SportsCapping Notifier

Windows desktop monitor for Brian Bitler's daily free picks. Sends real-time phone alerts with odds, rotation numbers, and game analysis.

⬇ Download Windows App (ZIP)
1. Download & Extract: Unzip the archive to your Desktop or Documents folder.
2. Install Phone App: Install ntfy on iOS or Android and subscribe to the topic shown in your dashboard.
3. Launch: Double-click SportsCapping_Notifier.exe and keep it running or minimized.
